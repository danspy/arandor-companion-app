What's new in the Arandor Companion App, newest first.

## October 2026

### Bestiary
- Stat blocks for monsters and NPCs, only for the DM: paste them from D&D Beyond (2024 Monster
  Manual) or type in your own, with a live preview
- Every number in a stat block rolls: attacks roll to hit and damage at once (a natural 20
  doubles the damage dice), plus saves, checks, skills, recharge and initiative
- Add monsters to a fight in one go: how many, average or your own HP, initiative rolled for
  each; NPCs with a stat block too
- The stat block opens next to the fight from the initiative tracker and the DM screen
- Damage and healing buttons on every combatant (players: on their own characters), also on
  the DM screen and next to an open stat block
- All 331 creatures of the free 2024 rules (SRD 5.2) ready to use, from guards and bandits to
  dragons
- Generate generic NPCs: species, role and level 1–20, computed with the player rules

### Dice: saved rolls
- Save your own rolls, like a weapon attack with to hit and damage, under *My rolls* on the
  dice roller page; they are on your account and one tap away in the dice button everywhere
- Advantage or disadvantage for the next saved roll; a natural 20 on the attack doubles the
  damage dice automatically
- Type any roll, e.g. `2d6+1d4+3` or `1W20+5`, in the dice button or on the dice roller page

### DM screen and player display
- Put party characters in the spotlight, not just NPCs: one list with both, their portraits
  look the same on the display
- Hide the location or single characters and NPCs from the players without removing them;
  Hide all / Show all and Clear for the whole spotlight
- Focus on some of them (or everyone): the display then shows only their portraits, big,
  without location, turn order or timer, which come back when the focus ends
- The picker only offers who is not in the spotlight yet
- Portraits glide smoothly to their new place whenever something changes, keep the location
  picture visible, and fit any number of characters on any screen; the turn order panel is
  only as tall as its list

### Quest pages
- Every quest has its own page with its diary entries, the NPCs, places and party members
  involved, and its timeline events
- Quests on the quest list, dashboard board and diary pages link to their page; NPC and
  location pages show their quests
- Library documents can be linked to quests; they appear under "From the Library" on the quest

### A new look
- One dark, immersive design across the whole app, with large scene images behind every page
- Smooth page transitions: pages zoom into details and slide between sections
- Themed dialogs instead of browser pop-ups, and better contrast everywhere
- Dark mode only: the light theme and its switch were removed

### Dashboard
- Welcome header with the next session countdown and "Add to my calendar"
- "Previously on Arandor…" recap of the last session, next to "Do you remember…?" with a
  random NPC, place, session or library document
- Party cards that flip to show ability scores; your own characters come first
- The Chronicle of earlier sessions, a quest board, the newest library documents, faction
  crests, and "The tale so far" with the campaign's numbers

### World map
- The world of Arandor is now drawn as a pixel-art world right in the browser
- The party walks its journey session by session, with story bubbles from the diary
- Journey log, travelled path, party members joining and leaving, and a playback timeline

### Dice everywhere
- A dice button on every page: quick d20, advantage, disadvantage, any mix of dice with a
  modifier, Reset, and press-and-hold for an instant d20
- 3D dice roll across the whole screen; natural 20s get sparks

### Installed app
- New bottom bar: your favourite pages, the dice as a gold seal in the middle, and a menu sheet
  with every page. Choose the bar's pages under Profile → Bottom bar pages

### Under the hood
- More secure logins: signed login cookies and stronger password protection
  (everyone had to log in once more)
- Newer foundation (Astro 7), a test site for every change before it goes live, and nightly
  backups that are kept forever
- The AI features (summaries, images, narration) were removed

### Fixes
- Calendar subscriptions show sessions at the right time (they were 1–2 hours late)
- "Delete summary" on a diary entry works again
- The player display link uses the real address and the player display has its styles back
